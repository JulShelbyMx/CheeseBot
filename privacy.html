Privacy Policy

CheeseBot

1. Data We May Collect

CheeseBot may process limited technical data needed to provide its features. Depending on the features you use, this may include Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, server settings, configuration data, and basic usage or error logs. The exact data processed depends on how the bot is configured and used.

2. How Data Is Used

This data is used to operate, maintain, troubleshoot, and improve CheeseBot and to save server-specific settings.

3. Message Content and Files

CheeseBot is not intended to store message content or personal files. However, some features or technical logs may process information temporarily when necessary to perform a requested action. Do not assume that information is never processed unless this has been verified against the bot's actual implementation.

4. Data Sharing and Service Providers

Personal data is not sold. Data may be handled by hosting, database, or other service providers when necessary to operate CheeseBot. Such providers may process data on the developer's behalf under their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Data is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Policy, subject to technical, operational, or legal requirements. Removing CheeseBot from a server does not necessarily delete all related data immediately. To ask about, access, or request deletion of data associated with you or your server, contact the developer through Discord.

6. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the CheeseBot developer through Discord.
